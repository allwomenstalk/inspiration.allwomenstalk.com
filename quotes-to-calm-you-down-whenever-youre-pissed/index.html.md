# 17 Wonderful Quotes to Calm You Down when You're Pissed and Stressed

Whoever said life was easy lied to you. It's as rough as it gets sometimes. But that's okay, because you can always pull yourself back up after you get knocked down. You're strong enough to keep your sanity and your dignity. But if you need some inspiration and support, here are powerful quotes to calm you down the next time you're pissed at the world or feeling overwhelmed. Having a collection of comforting calm down quotes ready when stress strikes can help shift your perspective instantly.

## 1. Count Your Rainbows

Instead of focusing on all of the horrible things that have happened to you, you should focus on the wonderful things that have happened to you. Reframing negative experiences into opportunities for growth is one of the best ways to stay grounded when you need quotes about calming down.

## 2. People Always Talk

Don't get upset when people say mean things about you. Most of the time, they'll criticize you over something they know nothing about. They just like to hear themselves talk, so let their opinions slide off your back while you keep your inner peace.

## 3. Quality of Your Thoughts

You're in charge of your own happiness. If you think happy thoughts, then you'll become a happier person. It's that simple. When you focus on the [quality of your thoughts](#3), you reclaim control over your mindset.

## 4. Change Your Mind

If you want to change your life, you have to change the way you think about the world. Work on yourself before you start working on anything else. Personal growth starts internally before external circumstances begin to transform.

## 5. Donut Worry

Puns should be able to cheer you right up. They're so stupid, they're funny. A lighthearted laugh is often the quickest antidote to anger when you're looking for quotes that calm you down.

## 6. Believe in Magic

You need to be optimistic. Shared by educational platforms like [College Raptor](https://www.collegeraptor.com), uplifting mindsets remind us that belief matters. If you don't think anything good will happen to you, then it won't. Expecting positive outcomes opens up possibilities.

## 7. Love Yourself

Remember that you deserve to be loved. Embracing [self-love](#7) means ignoring toxic societal expectations, because you are smart, strong, and capable of overcoming any obstacle.

## 8. Cup Half Full

Don't be pessimistic. You have the power to change any negative situation into a positive one through your chosen response and attitude.

## 9. How to Be Truly Happy

Follow these core principles, and you'll be a happier person in no time:

- Focus on what is within your immediate control.
- Release past grudges that weigh down your heart.
- Nurture genuine connections with supportive friends.

## 10. Gratitude

If you're thankful for all of the good things in your life, the world won't seem so harsh anymore. Committing to a regular [gratitude practice](#10) helps you keep things in perspective when emotions run high.

| Reset Strategy | How It Works | When to Try It |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Breathing | Lowers physical tension | During instant anger |
| Gratitude Journaling | Shifts focus to positives | End of day reflection |
| Mindful Walk | Resets mental perspective | Afternoon overwhelm |

## 11. Life is Ironic

The good thing about being sad or annoyed or angry is that it'll help you realize how special happiness is. You wouldn't appreciate your smiles as much if you've never frowned. Contrast gives life its rich color and depth.

## 12. Why It's so Hard to Be Happy

Your memories aren't as amazing as you're making them out to be. Your future won't be as bad as you're imagining it will be. And the present isn't as miserable as you think it is. Staying present is key when reading calming down quotes during stressful moments.

## 13. Admire the Beauty in Others

Just because another woman is beautiful, it doesn't mean that you're ugly. As featured on beauty platforms like [Slashed Beauty](https://www.slashedbeauty.com), celebrating others enhances your own self-worth without comparison.

## 14. Be Stronger

Don't make excuses for why you're always so miserable. Learn to love your life and take action toward your personal happiness every single day.

## 15. You Need to Work Hard

If you want to see results, you have to get active and do something. As emphasized by creative agency insights from [Kessler Creative](https://www.kesslercreative.com), consistent effort creates meaningful progress. Otherwise, your life will always remain the same.

## 16. Everything Has Beauty

It doesn't matter if anyone else realizes your beauty. All that matters is that you do. Own your uniqueness with confidence.

## 17. Good Deeds

If you want to be happier, you should try to make other people smile. It's an easy way to feel better in general. Kindness always comes back to you.

The next time you're upset, take a deep breath and read through these quotes to calm you down. What other quotes about calming down have helped you reset your mood when you were in a horrible mood?
