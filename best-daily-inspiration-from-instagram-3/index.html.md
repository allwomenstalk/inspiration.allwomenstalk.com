# 21 Best Daily Inspiration Accounts on Instagram for Women Seeking Positivity

Everyone needs a little extra encouragement and motivation to brighten their day. Whether you are navigating a difficult season, looking for daily mindset shifts, or simply wanting to fill your social feed with uplifting messages, curated social media creators offer quick, powerful doses of inspiration. Let this collection of **Instagram daily inspiration** creators turn things around and bring a genuine smile to your face.

- Fosters a daily habit of mindfulness and positive thinking

- Replaces mindless scrolling with meaningful micro-moments

- Offers quick reminders for self-care, mental wellness, and personal growth

## 1. @mikedooleytut

Mike Dooley is famous for reminding us that thoughts become things. His page offers daily notes from the universe that help shift your perspective toward possibility and gratitude, which complements the uplifting poetry found in [Cleo Wade's gentle reminders](#9). Check out the [official TUT portal](https://www.tut.com/) for more daily inspiration.

## 2. @stevemaraboli

Steve Maraboli delivers direct, empowering quotes about personal truth, resilience, and letting go of expectations. His posts act as a refreshing reality check whenever you need to regain your focus and inner strength.

## 3. @inspirationalqu0tes

This account curates timeless wisdom and bite-sized motivational quotes from thinkers and creators across the globe. It is an easy follow for anyone who appreciates a steady stream of positivity in their feed.

## 4. @the_mental_chef

Combining mental wellness with practical life wisdom, this creator serves up thought-provoking reminders to nourish your mind. Expect clean graphics and insightful reflections designed to spark self-reflection.

## 5. @rmdrk

R.M. Drake is celebrated for soulful poetry and reflective prose typed out on rustic paper background aesthetics. His words capture emotional nuance and pair beautifully with the daily reframing offered in [Vex King's mindset guides](#15).

## 6. @desiremanifestation

Focused on intentional living and law of attraction principles, this account provides daily prompts to help you align your goals with clear intention and positive action steps.

## 7. @motivational.daily.quotes

A classic hub for daily pep talks, offering visually striking typography paired with action-oriented quotes. Perfect for starting your morning on a proactive note.

## 8. @millionaire_motto

Geared toward ambition, career success, and financial discipline, this account shares daily mantras to keep your long-term goals top of mind while building healthy work habits.

## 9. @cleowade

Artist and author Cleo Wade writes heartwarming handwritten poems that feel like a warm hug from a friend. Visit the [official website of Cleo Wade](https://www.cleowade.com/) to explore more of her books and art.

## 10. @motivation_mondays

You don't have to wait until Monday to get inspired. This page serves consistent reminders that every day is a fresh opportunity to reset your energy and start again.

## 11. @youareluminous

Radiating positive vibrations, this space focuses on spiritual growth, self-love, and emotional healing with gentle aesthetics and grounding words.

## 12. @bethesilverlining

Dedicated to highlighting optimism even in challenging times, this creator reminds us to look for hope and light in every situation.

## 13. @mindfulmft

Run by a licensed marriage and family therapist, this feed offers deep insights into relationship dynamics, self-compassion, and emotional health, working hand-in-hand with [body positivity accounts](#21) that celebrate self-acceptance.

## 14. @law_of_positivity_

Offers gentle guidance on mindfulness practices, energy healing, and breaking free from negative thought cycles with clear, digestible posts.

## 15. @vexking

Best-selling author Vex King shares powerful wisdom on self-love, high-vibrational living, and overcoming adversity. Discover more of his books on [Vex King's official website](https://vexking.com/).

## 16. @laurenmcaulay_

Sharing personal growth journeys and relatable life lessons, Lauren's page encourages authentic self-expression and personal empowerment.

## 17. @mastinkipp

Trauma-informed creator and author Mastin Kipp provides actionable insights on personal transformation and purpose-driven living. Explore his programs at [Mastin Kipp's official portal](https://mastinkipp.com/).

## 18. @happsters

Dedicated entirely to spreading happiness, this page posts uplifting quotes, adorable visuals, and gentle daily reminders to appreciate the small moments.

## 19. @powerofpositivity

One of the largest positivity communities online, offering inspiring stories, wellness tips, and daily encouragement to keep your spirit lifted.

## 20. @thegoodquote

A renowned platform advocating for mental health awareness and self-care through curated quotes from writers, poets, and advocates around the world.

## 21. @positivebodyimage

Empowering individuals to embrace their bodies with self-love and confidence, this account challenges conventional beauty standards with uplifting messages.

Feeling inspired yet? We sure are! Don't forget to share your favorite inspiring Instagram accounts with us.
