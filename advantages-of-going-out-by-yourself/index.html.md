# 7 Surprising Advantages of Going out by Yourself \(And Why It’s Not Weird\)

Going out with friends is a blast, but have you ever considered the freedom of a solo date? Many women feel it’s weird to go out alone, fearing the judgment of others. However, learning [how to be happy alone](https://inspiration.allwomenstalk.com/how-to-be-happy-alone/) is a superpower. Whether it’s hitting the cinema or dining solo, there are actually several benefits to going out on your own. If you've been wondering, "is it normal to go out by yourself?" the answer is a resounding yes!

Here are some quick ideas for your next solo outing:

- A mid-day matinee at the local cinema.

- A quiet afternoon at a botanical garden.

- Dinner at that fancy bistro you've been eyeing.

- A visit to a new art exhibition.

## 1. Spontaneous

Making arrangements to go out with friends can be a hassle, especially if you're meeting up as a group. You have to agree on a time, place and what you're going to do. Going out on your own means that you can be completely spontaneous. You can go out if you feel like it, or change your mind about what you want to do. It's totally flexible—perfect for those days when you just need a change of scenery without the back-and-forth.

## 2. Relaxing

Going out on your own can also be more relaxing than having company. You don't have to worry about keeping anyone else happy, or deal with their complaints about their life, relationship, or job. Instead, you can please yourself. You can go where you want and do what you like, rather than having to miss out because the other person or people don't want to go there. It’s the ultimate form of [self-care](https://inspiration.allwomenstalk.com/self-care-habits-for-busy-women/).

| Feature | Group Outing | Solo Outing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Decision Making | Consensus-based | Instant & personal |
| Flexibility | Rigid schedule | Completely spontaneous |
| Social Energy | High (required) | Low (relaxing) |
| Cost | Potential for split bills | Full control over budget |

## 3. Easy to Choose

Have you ever arranged to go to the movies with a friend, only to find that choosing the film takes longer than the movie itself? She's seen the one you want to see, you don't like the genre she wants to see, and the one that you both want to see is on when you're at work. Going out alone makes it so easy to choose what you do! You can even check out reviews on [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com) and pick exactly what fits your mood.

## 4. Budget

Going out on your own can also be easier on your budget. Going out as a group often means that you end up subsidising other people, or spending more than you intended. Perhaps you end up eating out afterwards when you'd rather go home. And if you have a flexible timetable, you can go to the cinema during the day, when it's cheaper and your friends are at work. You can find great matinee deals on [Fandango](https://www.fandango.com).

## 5. Own Company

Some people are happy with their own company, and there's nothing wrong with that. So don't feel obliged to go out with other people; if you want to go out on your own, then go right ahead. You're doing what you want, and you shouldn't feel you have to socialise if you're not in the mood for it. It's about [building self-confidence](https://inspiration.allwomenstalk.com/tips-to-build-your-self-confidence/) and being comfortable in your own skin.

## 6. Easy Getting a Ticket

If you want to go see a popular show, you may find it easier to get a single ticket. Most people are going with friends and want to sit together, which leaves odd seats dotted about the theater. So you could actually have a better choice of seats than if you were looking for two or more seats. Sites like [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com) often have "single seat" options that are much closer to the stage.

## 7. Meeting Potential Partners

If you're single, going out alone makes it more likely that you'll get chatting to potential partners. Guys may not want to approach you if you're with friends. You're also more likely to meet the kind of guy you get on with, as you'll already have an interest in common. If you’re looking to meet new people in a structured way, [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com) is also a fantastic resource for solo explorers.

So don't be held back because you're worried that people will think it's strange to be out alone. They probably won't even notice. Just enjoy your own company; take a book for quiet moments or look at your phone. If you've been asking yourself, "is it weird to go out alone?" remember that the most confident people are often those seen enjoying their own company. So, where are you heading on your next solo adventure?
