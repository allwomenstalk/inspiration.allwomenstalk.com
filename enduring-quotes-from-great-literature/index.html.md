# 7 Enduring Quotes from Literature That Will Stay with You Forever

Whether or not you're unable to find the words to express your feelings, learning a few enduring quotes from literature can make all the difference. These timeless sentiments have the power to light up someone's day or give a loved one that gentle push of encouragement when they need it most. From reflections on romance to profound wisdom on overcoming adversity, classic books offer an unending well of inspiration. The quotes in this collection represent the works of some of the world's greatest writers, each leaving a lasting impact on readers across generations.

Here is why classic literary lines continue to resonate so deeply:

- Expressing complex emotions when your own words fail
- Providing comfort and perspective during challenging moments
- Connecting us to universal human experiences across centuries

Without further ado, let's explore seven enduring quotes from literature that continue to move and inspire us today.

## 1. From Anna Karenina

![From Anna Karenina](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/d1/yv/yuaoca7f_340x193.jpg)

To begin this list of enduring quotes, let's start with Leo Tolstoy's masterpiece, _Anna Karenina_. When Count Vronsky looks at Anna and thinks, _'He stepped down, trying not to look long at her, as if she were the sun, yet he saw her, like the sun, even without looking,'_ it captures the overwhelming nature of romance. Who wouldn't want the person they love to use such a beautiful quote to describe them? It is so sweet and amazing. Tolstoy has several more expressions that you will also love, available to explore on [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1399). Some feel like a love that just isn't meant to be in certain instances; yet others seem as though our deepest thoughts were written specifically for us, much like the family bonds explored in [Little Women](#4).

## 2. From Romeo and Juliet

![From Romeo and Juliet](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/zp/po/e2a6nuup_403x275.jpg)

It really doesn't matter which version of the play you watch; it remains utterly wonderful and romantic. Yes, I know it centers on star-crossed teenagers and carries that tragic ending. However, lines like _'My bounty is as boundless as the sea, my love as deep; the more I give to thee, the more I have, for both are infinite'_ flow so elegantly and never fail to captivate audiences. I have a great admiration for William Shakespeare's work, as he is among the classic writers who have deeply influenced my own writing.

## 3. From Twelfth Night

![From Twelfth Night](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/tu/pv/nnlu4me3_375x352.jpg)

This is by far one of my favorite Shakespeare quotes: _'If music be the food of love, play on.'_ The comedy _Twelfth Night_ offers so many memorable lines as it unfolds the tale of Viola, a woman disguised as a man who falls madly in love with Duke Orsino while helping him woo another noblewoman. If you ever find yourself in a tangled romantic situation and need help expressing your feelings, you're sure to find the right sentiment within this play. You can delve into complete performance archives and scholarly guides through the [Folger Shakespeare Library](https://www.folger.edu/). If you appreciate sharp comedic wit, you might also enjoy the romantic misunderstandings found in [Northanger Abbey](#5).

## 4. From Little Women

![From Little Women](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/op/s7/jng63wfx_500x333.jpg)

Louisa May Alcott's _Little Women_ brings us heartwarming reflections on family, sisterhood, and romantic devotion. This classic quote carries the sentiment that when you are truly in love, you radiate an inner glow that is uniquely beautiful. The shared struggles faced by Meg, Jo, Beth, and Amy March draw them closer together as a family. If you and your loved ones are tight-knit, sharing Little Women love quotes could present you with the perfect message for a handwritten card or framed artwork during the holidays.

| Literary Masterpiece | Core Sentiment | Perfect Occasion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anna Karenina | Unspoken, captivating romance | Anniversary cards |
| Twelfth Night | Joyful passion for music and love | Romantic celebrations |
| Little Women | Warmth, family devotion, and inner beauty | Holiday greetings & family notes |

## 5. From Northanger Abbey

![From Northanger Abbey](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/pj/n9/f22e6szo_500x375.jpg)

In _Northanger Abbey_, Jane Austen writes: _'There is nothing I would not do for those who are really my friends. I have no notion of loving people by halves, it is not my nature.'_ This quote provides a wondrous sentiment for the devoted friend who has always stood by your side. After all, it is usually our best friend who helps us through breakups, supports our life changes, and brings over comfort foods like blueberry cream pie or ice cream when we desperately need it most. To discover more about Austen's life and work, visit [Jane Austen's House](https://janeaustens.house/) online museum.

## 6. From David Copperfield

![From David Copperfield](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/rv/ra/c9le5qbx_600x323.jpg)

Charles Dickens' _David Copperfield_ is filled with profound reflections on resilience and personal growth. These David Copperfield quotes are inspiring and possess real power to help others navigate hard times. Offering words of hope in dire circumstances can give a dear friend the encouragement to persevere when faced with adversity. I love Dickens' books, which are celebrated by institutions like the [Charles Dickens Museum](https://dickensmuseum.com/), because they instill motivation and drive even in challenging situations. Much like the moral strength found in [To Kill a Mockingbird](#7), his writing reminds us never to give up hope.

## 7. From to Kill a Mockingbird

![From to Kill a Mockingbird](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/hk/pf/jctbgm15_500x300.jpg)

As a writer from Alabama, I must say that Harper Lee is, in fact, a treasure to us all. Her iconic wisdom—_'You never really understand a person until you consider things from his point of view... until you climb into his skin and walk around in it'_—has inspired generations to practice empathy and take a stand against prejudice. While justice and equality remain ongoing pursuits, many positive changes have evolved over the decades. I have been inspired by her words throughout my life and take this lesson into consideration on a daily basis.

Great literature provides us with invaluable life lessons, from processing love gained and lost to championing meaningful societal change. What are some of your favorite quotes from classic literature?
