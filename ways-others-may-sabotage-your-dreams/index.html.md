# Why Your Dreams Aren't Working and How to Fix It: Dealing with Sabotage

If you have dreams that you want to follow but you feel as though there might be people who are trying to stop you from achieving them, you might not be imagining it. There are several ways in which people might try and sabotage your dreams, and while I'm not saying you should be paranoid, it's essential to understand why do people sabotage others. Understanding these patterns helps you protect your peace while you work to [make your dreams real](https://inspiration.allwomenstalk.com/how-to-stay-motivated-to-reach-your-goals/). Here are several things you should be aware of and some ways to deal with those who might try and stand in your way.

## 1. Negativity

One of the ways others might try and sabotage your dreams is by being negative. If you have a Negative Nancy or Nathan in your life, take their negativity with a pinch of salt. They might try and tell you that your ambitions aren't worth pursuing, often focusing on the downsides. It may be that they never had the strength to pursue their own dreams and they're trying to stand in your way. Of course, they might be trying to protect you from getting hurt, but they also need to accept that you need to find things out for yourself. The key is to surround yourself with positive people who are [pursuing the same goals](https://inspiration.allwomenstalk.com/ways-to-stay-positive-when-life-is-getting-you-down/).

## 2. Mocking

Another way people might try and sabotage your dreams is by mocking you. They might make fun of your hopes and aspirations for the future by belittling your vision and making you doubt yourself. Stay strong in the face of mockery; laugh and go along with it if you can, but don't take it personally. Often, this behavior is a projection of their own insecurities or jealousy. Remember that [dream stealers](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/the-squeaky-wheel/201309/the-psychology-dream-stealers) often use mockery to mask their own fear of failure.

## 3. Anecdotes

People might tell you horror stories of someone they knew who pursued a similar path and ended up struggling. There are risks with everything in life, and while these anecdotes should be listened to, they shouldn't be taken to heart. Don't let their fear stand in the way of your progress. Every journey is unique, and someone else's failure doesn't dictate your success.

## 4. Interference

Interference is a more direct way others might try to sabotage your dreams. This can take many forms, from providing incorrect information to intentionally creating distractions. Be aware of the ways in which others might interfere with your schedule or resources. Staying organized and keeping your plans private can often prevent this kind of [external obstacle](#{7}) from slowing you down.

## 5. Psychological Games

This is one of the more subtle ways people who sabotage others operate. They might play psychological games to make you feel as though your dream is not worth the effort or that you're better off staying exactly where you are. These tactics can be manipulative, but the best defense is to stay grounded and listen to your inner voice. If you've ever had a [dream about someone sabotaging me](https://www.verywellmind.com/what-do-dreams-about-being-chased-mean-2795924), it might be your subconscious alerting you to these real-life dynamics.

## 6. Being Mean

Some people can just be mean-spirited and like to make others feel small because it makes them feel more powerful. Try to distance yourself from toxic individuals who make you feel silly about pursuing your goals. Surround yourself with a community that celebrates your wins instead of tearing them down.

## 7. Obstacles

Finally, people might try and put physical or logistical obstacles in your way. For example, if you need a specific resource for an interview or a project, it might conveniently become unavailable right when you need it. Whatever the scenario, always have a back-up plan—and a back-up plan for your back-up plan! Being prepared for potential [interference](#{4}) ensures that your momentum remains steady.

| Method of Sabotage | Primary Impact | Best Defense |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Negativity | Lowers motivation | Seek positive circles |
| Mocking | Increases self-doubt | Develop thick skin |
| Interference | Causes delays | Keep plans private |

Dealing with people who sabotage others is a common hurdle on the path to success. Has anyone been on the receiving end of this? What did you do, and what advice would you give to others who are dealing with those trying to dim their light?
